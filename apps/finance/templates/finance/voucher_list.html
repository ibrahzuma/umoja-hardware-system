Vouchers

Every entry in the books starts as a voucher: a Sales or Purchase voucher for an invoice, a Receipt for money in, a Payment for money out, a Contra to move our own money, a Journal for anything else. Each one is posted to the General Ledger the moment it balances.

One row per voucher line. Rows sharing a Ref are one voucher, so debits and credits of a Ref must add up. Sales and Purchase rows also take the Party, Invoice No and EFD No; a Receipt or Payment row can name the invoice it is Against.

The optional Ledgers sheet creates ledgers (with opening balances) before the vouchers post. The Ledger List sheet is your current chart of accounts — copy the codes or exact names from it.
